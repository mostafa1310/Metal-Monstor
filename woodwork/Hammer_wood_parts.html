Hammer stick + head – laser cut

Only the stick and the head are wood. They are 2 flat shapes, both cut from 10 mm plywood: 2 × the stick T, glued face to face → a 20 × 20 stick, and 2 side plates, one glued on each side of the head → a 40 mm head. Finished sizes: stick 20 × 20, head 64 × 25 × 40. The motor upright, the hub and the stop posts are 3D printed (stl/P7 hub with a 20.3 mm slot, P8 upright, P9 / P10 stop posts).

The 2 shapes (real size at 100 %) – cut each twice – and gluing

  1. Glue the 2 stick Ts face to face (thin even layer of wood glue). Push M4 bolts through the 3 holes to line them up, clamp along the whole length, let dry → 20 × 20 stick.
  2. Glue the 2 side plates on both sides of the wide end, M4 bolt through the head hole to line them up, clamp, let dry. The head is now 64 × 25 × 40.
  3. When dry: M4×50 + nyloc through the head. Sand the edges.
  4. Top 30 mm of the stick into the printed hub slot (20.3 × 20.3) with epoxy, 2 × M4×40 through hub + stick.
