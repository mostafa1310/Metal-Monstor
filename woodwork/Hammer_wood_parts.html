How to read the drawings

All numbersare millimetres (mm). 10 mm = 1 cm.
Red arrowsa measurement: the number is the distance between the two arrow tips.
A B …a hole. The table under the drawing says which drill bit and how deep.
- - - dashed linesomething on the other side of the wood (you can't see it from this side).
Real sizeprint the pages at 100 % ("actual size", not "fit to page"). Then the drawing is exactly as big as the part – you can cut it out, glue it on the wood and drill through the marks. Check: the black-and-white bar must measure 50 mm with a ruler.
Please do not drill holes in the stick or the head – we drill those ourselves when we put everything together, so they line up.

1 · Motor plate – holes and steps

HoleHow manyDrill bitHow deepWhere
A126 mm flat-bottom bit (Forstner)9 mm only – do NOT go throughfrom the BACK side, in the middle (40 from each side), 58 up from the bottom
B18 mmall the way throughin the middle of hole A
C23.5 mmthrough8.5 above and 8.5 below the middle of A. On the FRONT side widen the top a little (countersink) so a flat screw head sits flush
D24.5 mmthrough12 up from the bottom, 15 in from each end
E23 mm25 mm deepinto the bottom edge, 10 in from each end, in the middle of the 12 mm thickness

Steps

  1. Cut the shape from 12 mm plywood: 80 wide at the bottom, 50 wide at the top, 78 tall (see page 2). Sand the edges.
  2. Mark the holes (or glue the real-size page 2 on the wood).
  3. Turn the plate over to the BACK side. Drill hole A with the 26 mm flat-bottom bit, only 9 mm deep – stop when 3 mm of wood is left (tape on the drill bit at 9 mm helps).
  4. Drill B (8 mm) through the middle of A.
  5. Drill the 2 holes C (3.5 mm) through. On the FRONT side, widen their top with a countersink bit (or a 7 mm bit, just 2 mm).
  6. Drill the 2 holes D (4.5 mm) through.
  7. Stand the plate on its bottom edge and drill the 2 holes E (3 mm) straight down, 25 mm deep.

2 · Stick   and   3 · Two small blocks

2 · Stick – 1 piece

  1. Hard wood (beech or oak), straight grain, no knots.
  2. Cut a strip 20 mm × 15 mm.
    Exactly 20 × 15, or a tiny bit smaller (19.8 × 14.8) – not bigger: the top end must slide into a plastic part with a 20 × 15 hole.
  3. Cut it to 222.5 mm long, square ends.
  4. No holes – we drill them ourselves.

3 · Two small blocks – 2 pieces

  1. Cut 2 blocks, both 44 long × 12 thick: block 1 is 14.5 tall, block 2 is 25 tall.
  2. In each: 2 holes F 3.5 mm, from the top straight through to the bottom, 7 mm from each end, in the middle of the 12 mm.
  3. Glue a piece of 3.5 mm rubber (44 × 12) on top.

4 · Hammer head – made from 4 glued pieces

Gluing 4 simple pieces is easier than cutting a square hole into one block. Use hard wood; all pieces are 25 mm tall.

PieceHow manySize (long × tall × thick)
1 and 2 – long pieces264 × 25 × 7.5
3 and 4 – small blocks222 × 25 × 15
Rubber pads228 × 23, 3 mm thick

Steps

  1. Cut the 4 pieces. All of them exactly 25 mm tall.
  2. Glue: long piece – 2 small blocks at the two ends – long piece (like a sandwich, see the drawing). The gap in the middle must be a hole 20 × 15 going all the way through, top to bottom. Put the stick in the hole while the glue dries so it stays the right size (do not glue the stick!).
  3. Clamp and let it dry. Sand the outside flat: 64 × 30 × 25.
  4. Glue a rubber pad on each 30 × 25 end.
  5. No other holes – we drill the bolt hole ourselves.

Finished? Please check

  • Motor plate: 80 wide, 78 tall, 12 thick. Hole A is only 9 mm deep (3 mm of wood left) and on the back side.
  • Motor plate: holes C are countersunk on the front side (the side without hole A).
  • Motor plate: 2 small holes E in the bottom edge.
  • Stick: 20 × 15 (not bigger), 222.5 long, no holes.
  • Stick slides into the hole of the head without forcing.
  • Head: 64 × 30 × 25, rubber on both ends, 20 × 15 hole through the middle.
  • 2 blocks: 14.5 and 25 tall, 2 holes each, rubber on top.

How we will use the parts

The motor (blue) sits in the 26 mm hole of the plate; 2 metal angles bolt the plate to the robot.

A 3D-printed part (orange) goes on the motor; the stick (blue) slides into it and we bolt it.

Thank you! If something is not clear, call us before cutting – it is easier to ask than to make it again.
