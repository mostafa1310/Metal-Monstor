Hammer stick + head – laser cut

Only the stick and the head are wood. They are 2 flat shapes, both cut from 6 mm plywood (the laser's maximum): 2 × the stick T, glued face to face → a 12 mm stick, and 2 side plates, one glued on each side of the head → a 24 mm head. Finished sizes: stick 20 × 12, head 64 × 25 × 24. The motor upright, the hub and the stop posts are 3D printed (stl/P7 hub with a 12.3 mm slot, P8 upright, P9 / P10 stop posts).

The 2 shapes (real size at 100 %) – cut each twice – and gluing

  1. Glue the 2 stick Ts face to face (thin even layer of wood glue). Push M4 bolts through the 3 holes to line them up, clamp along the whole length, let dry → 12 mm stick.
  2. Glue the 2 side plates on both sides of the wide end, M4 bolt through the head hole to line them up, clamp, let dry. The head is now 64 × 25 × 24.
  3. When dry: M4×30 + nyloc through the head. Sand the edges.
  4. Glue a rubber pad on each 25 × 24 end of the head.
  5. Top 30 mm of the stick into the printed hub slot (12.3 × 20) with epoxy, 2 × M4×35 through hub + stick.
